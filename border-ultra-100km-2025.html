02 / My endurance journey

From preparation to the finish.

In December 2025, I completed The Border 100 km at the Jaisalmer–Laungewala event. My official finish time was 15 hours, 45 minutes and 51 seconds.

The photographs preserve the journey: collecting bib 100205, getting ready with fellow runners, moving through the daytime road course and celebrating with a finisher medal at night.

This 100 km finish is a lasting milestone in my running journey, alongside the triathlon chapter that followed at IRONMAN 70.3 Goa in 2026.
