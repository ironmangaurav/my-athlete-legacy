About Me

An endurance athlete from Agra, building a lasting record of movement, discipline and personal growth.

Gaurav Yadav

My sporting journey brings together running, cycling and triathlon. I completed The Border 100 km in December 2025 in 15:45:51. Completing IRONMAN 70.3 Goa in November 2026 marked another important chapter.

I value persistence, patient preparation and the lessons that come from difficult days. This archive preserves the real photographs, records and memories behind the milestones.

The next goal is Fit at 50: becoming stronger, more mobile and healthier through consistent habits, while keeping family life at the centre.
